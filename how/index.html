つかいかたhow to use ジャプリ

Five things worth knowing once. They are the same in every app on ジャプリ.

日本語をタイプするTurn the Japanese keyboard on

Several apps ask you to type in Japanese. A laptop will not do it until you add the Japanese keyboard, and you only have to do it once. The steps below are for the laptop you are reading this on, and the box at the end tells you whether it worked.

ホームがめんPut an app on your phone

Save any ジャプリ app to your home screen and it opens like an app: full screen, its own icon, no address bar. It is still the website, so it updates itself and takes almost no room on the phone.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the app in Safari. This does not work from a link inside Instagram, Messages or another app's own browser.
  2. Tap Share, the square with an arrow coming out of the top. It is at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone and at the top on an iPad.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Change the name if you want a shorter one, then tap Add.

Using Chrome on an iPhone? Tap the same Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Older iPhones put Share in the menu behind the three dots.

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Install or Add. Some phones show Install app straight away in that menu.
  4. Drag the icon where you want it, or tap Add automatically.

On a Samsung using Samsung Internet, tap the three lines at the bottom right, then Add page to, then Home screen.

Do it once for each app you use often. The hub itself can go on there too, which gives you all of them behind one icon.

てんすうWhere your score lives

Each app keeps your points and your ticks in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody else can see it.

  • Close the app and come back: your score is still there.
  • Use a different laptop, or a private window, and you start again.
  • Reset clears that app on that device, and cannot be undone.
  • Save copies a line of text you can paste to your teacher.

うごきかたGetting around

  • Tap ジャプリ at the top left of any app to come back to the hub.
  • Each app remembers the tab you were last on and opens there.
  • A link from your teacher can open one tab straight away, so it lands where the lesson starts.
  • The sign that flashes up when an app opens is only a welcome. Tap anywhere to go straight past it.

ぜんがめんFill the screen

Tap the four corners button at the top of any app to hide the tabs, the address bar and the bookmarks. Only the app is left. Tap it again, or press Esc, to get the browser back.

  • Use it for a test or a timed task, so nothing else is one tap away.
  • Going back to ジャプリ ends full screen. Open the next app, then tap the button again.
  • iPhones cannot do this in Safari, so the button does not appear there. Save the app to your home screen instead, as above: it opens with no browser around it.

きりかえTurning things on and off

  • ふりがな hides or shows the kana above the kanji. Turn it off when you want to test yourself.
  • EN hides or shows the English.
  • ♪ turns the sounds off.

Each switch stays as you leave it on that device.

ふるいページIf a page looks out of date

Every app prints its build in the bottom right corner. If what you see does not match what your teacher describes, your browser is holding an old copy: hold Shift and press reload, or add ?welcome=1 to the address, which asks for a fresh page.
