つかいかたhow to use ジャプリ

Five things worth knowing once. They are the same in every app on ジャプリ.

日本語をタイプするTurn the Japanese keyboard on

Several apps ask you to type in Japanese. A laptop will not do it until you add the Japanese keyboard, and you only have to do it once. The steps below are for the laptop you are reading this on, and the box at the end tells you whether it worked.

てんすうWhere your score lives

Each app keeps your points and your ticks in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody else can see it.

  • Close the app and come back: your score is still there.
  • Use a different laptop, or a private window, and you start again.
  • Reset clears that app on that device, and cannot be undone.
  • Save copies a line of text you can paste to your teacher.

うごきかたGetting around

  • Tap ジャプリ at the top left of any app to come back to the hub.
  • Each app remembers the tab you were last on and opens there.
  • A link from your teacher can open one tab straight away, so it lands where the lesson starts.
  • The sign that flashes up when an app opens is only a welcome. Tap anywhere to go straight past it.

きりかえTurning things on and off

  • ふりがな hides or shows the kana above the kanji. Turn it off when you want to test yourself.
  • EN hides or shows the English.
  • ♪ turns the sounds off.

Each switch stays as you leave it on that device.

ふるいページIf a page looks out of date

Every app prints its build in the bottom right corner. If what you see does not match what your teacher describes, your browser is holding an old copy: hold Shift and press reload, or add ?welcome=1 to the address, which asks for a fresh page.
